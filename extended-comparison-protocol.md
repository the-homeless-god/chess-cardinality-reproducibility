# Extended comparisons, version 1 (11 October 2026)

These are exploratory additions decided after the earlier March–May results were known. No new confirmatory claim or pre-registration is implied. No April or May label is used for fitting, model selection, stopping or choosing a seed.

1. March ablations: seeds 100–109; fixed-configuration GA2 with the same validation ≥0.90 / three-generation plateau rule as GA0; random weight search in [-2,2]^6, 600 training evaluations grouped in 50 batches of 12, one validation evaluation of the best training candidate in each batch. Maximum train/validation budgets match GA2. Report test accuracy and informative subset, evaluations, fitting time, and paired position bootstrap. Simple early stopping can stop before 396 evaluations; this is an ablation of the hierarchy's overhead.
2. April: reuse all 250 independently selected roots and saved exact labels. Evaluate all ten frozen March cascade seeds, greedy material, and Stockfish 17.1 at 1,000 and 10,000 nodes per decision. Engine: Threads=1, Hash=16 MiB, Clear Hash before every decision, no opening book or Syzygy path. Exact labels are the reference, not Stockfish evaluations. Node budgets enable repeatable effort but are not equal wall-time budgets for Python policies.
3. May: exclude all roots already terminal under `outcome(claim_draw=True)` (expected five); preserve every other root and predecessor context. Pair candidate sides against Stockfish at 1,000 nodes. Candidates: observed, inferred, fixed priority, frozen GA seed 100, min-κ, greedy material. Same 250 ms safety ceiling for Python policies as before; 200-ply cutoffs are reported separately and treated as draws in the primary descriptive score. Report draws/cutoffs, latency, paired root confidence intervals and baseline score from the root's exact WDL. These are continuations from selected endgames, not starting-position tournaments or Elo estimates.
4. Frozen policy chosen by seed 100, not best test seed. Bootstrap seed 20261011, 10,000 resamples of independent root positions; average across algorithm seeds before resampling. Time results depend on hardware and interpreter; record environment and binary SHA-256.
5. Record root/move cursed-win and blessed-loss frequencies and DTZ availability; do not infer full-game 50-move correctness from reset-clock root labels. No extension to arbitrary seven-piece positions, all endgames, or all openings is claimed.
